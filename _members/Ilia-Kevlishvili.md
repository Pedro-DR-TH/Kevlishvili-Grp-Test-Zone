Ilia Kevlishvili is an Assistant Professor of Chemistry and Biochemistry at Baylor University, a position he began in August 2025. His research sits at the intersection of computational chemistry, machine learning, and catalysis, with a specific focus on developing novel string-based representations (such as T-REX) for inorganic and organometallic complexes.
