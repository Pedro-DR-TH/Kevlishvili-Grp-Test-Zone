Dr. Anil K. Katwal is a computational science researcher specializing in complex systems, physical informed neural networks(PINNs), and energy based generative models. His expertise brides statistical mechanics and AI, with a research spanning spin glass system, global optimization strategies ,  and diffusion model. He actively integrates quantum machines learning framework( Cirq and Qiskit), to enhance scalable machine learning workflows.

In Dr. llia’s research group, Dr. Katwal focuses on data-efficient machine learning models for transitions metal complexes, aiming to drastically lower time complexity and computational costs in quantum chemistry and materials discovery.
