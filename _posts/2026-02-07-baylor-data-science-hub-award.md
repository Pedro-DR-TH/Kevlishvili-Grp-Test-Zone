We are honored to announce that the Kevlishvili Group has been named a recipient of the Baylor Data Science Hub Affiliate Award. This recognition highlights our group's commitment to developing data-driven tools for chemical discovery and reflects the interdisciplinary nature of our work at the intersection of chemistry, AI, and data science. We are grateful for the support and look forward to continued collaboration with the Baylor Data Science Hub.
